# Green Software Engineering: Reducing the Environmental Impact of AI

## 1. What Is Green Software Engineering?

Its main objectives include:
- Reducing energy consumption and carbon emissions.
- Improving software and hardware resource efficiency.
- Minimizing unnecessary computing and data storage.
- Reducing electronic waste and extending hardware lifespans.


## 2. Energy Efficiency Challenges in Modern Computing

The growing demand for cloud computing, data centres, and artificial intelligence (AI) creates several environmental challenges.

**Current problems with AI**
-high energy usage 
-power spikes
-wasted heat
-electronic waste
-water consumption

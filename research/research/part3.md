# Research notes: Part 3, what developers can do

## Green Software Foundation key areas
- **Energy efficiency:** "Use the least amount of energy possible."
- **Carbon awareness:** "Do more when the electricity is cleaner and do less when the electricity is dirtier."
- **Hardware efficiency:** "Use the least amount of embodied carbon possible."
- **Measurement:** "What you can't measure, you can't improve."
- Source: Green Software Foundation, https://learn.greensoftware.foundation/introduction

## Measuring software's carbon
- The Software Carbon Intensity (SCI) specification became an ISO standard, announced on April 25, 2024.
- Source: https://greensoftware.foundation/articles/sci-specification-achieves-iso-standard-status/
